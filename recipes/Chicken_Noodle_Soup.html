Chicken Noodle Soup

Home

Description

Chicken Noodle Soup is a hearty delicious meal. Great for cool winter nights and for getting over a flu.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Olive Oil
  • Chicken Breasts
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Mustard Powder
  • Italian Seasoning
  • Dill
  • Parsely
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Carrots
  • Celery

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of water to boil
  2. Add the pasta to the water
  3. Continiue to boil pasta until it is cooked to your likeness, I prefer just a bit past al dente
  4. Add Olive Oil to a pre-heated pot
  5. While the oil heats up, dice the Onion, Celery, Garlic, and Carrots and set aside
  6. Add the chicken breasts to the pot
  7. Season both sides of the chicken with Salt, Pepper, Onion Powder, Garlic Powder, Mustard Powder, Italian Seasoning, Dill, and Parsely
  8. Sear both sides of the chicken
  9. Remove the chicken from the pot and add the diced veggies
  10. Cover the pot and dice the chicken while the veggies cook
  11. Continue to cook the veggies until the onions turn golden brown
  12. Add the diced chicken to the pot
  13. Add the pasta to the pot
  14. Add the broth to the pot
  15. Season the soup with Salt, Pepper, Onion Powder, Garlic Powder, Mustard Powder, Italian Seasoning, Dill, and Parsely
