Steak

Home

Description

Steak is delicious and very healthy when prepared properly.

Ingredients

  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Mustard Powder
  • Italian Seasoning
  • Lime Juice
  • Top Of Iowa Steak
  • Onion
  • Mushrooms

Steps

  1. Place a layer of olive oil into a pre-heated skillet
  2. While the oil heats up, dice the onion and set it aside
  3. Add the Steak to the skillet
  4. Season both sides of the steak with Salt, Pepper, Onion Powder, Garlic Powder, Mustard Powder, and Italian Seasoning
  5. Heavily sear each side of the steak
  6. Remove the steak from the skillet
  7. Add the onions and mushrooms to the skillet
  8. Cut the steak into strips
  9. Continue to cook the onions until they are golden brown
  10. Add the steak to the skillet
  11. Add lime juice to the skillet and turn off the heat
  12. Let sit for 5 minutes
  13. Serve with roasted potatoes or rice
